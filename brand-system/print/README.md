# Print, signage, field, and apparel artwork

These files use the approved vector identity. They are layout/production inputs, not evidence that a printer, sign fabricator, or embroidery shop has approved materials or color.

## Business card

`Business_Card.pdf` has two pages: front, then back. Trim is **3.5 × 2 inches** with **0.125-inch bleed** on every edge. PDF MediaBox/BleedBox are 3.75 × 2.25 inches; TrimBox is inset 0.125 inch. The back uses Adam Abdalla and cypresscommand.com; `[Role]`, `[Email]`, and `[Phone]` must be replaced before ordering.

`Business_Card.html` is the editable layout. Open it locally in Edge/Chrome and click the name/contact fields to edit. Browser edits are temporary unless the source is saved. Edit the HTML source for persistent changes. Print with backgrounds enabled, no browser headers/footers, and 100% scaling using its CSS page size. Browser exports alone may not retain the explicit PDF TrimBox; have the printer set/verify the 3.5 × 2-inch trim before production. Do not send the placeholder PDF as a final order.

Embroidery artwork is black as a single-color separation, not a mandatory black thread selection. Use Cypress thread on light fabric or Bone/white thread on dark fabric, subject to material samples. The symbol's 25mm height is a starting baseline, not a guarantee of stitch quality. Vendor digitizing/DST production and stitch testing are still required. Do not import a picture of embroidery as production artwork.

Before ordering, confirm actual dimensions, material, placement, mounting, substrate, legibility, and vendor proof. Leave the required clear space around the visible mark. For helmet/field decals, obtain manufacturer approval for adhesive/material compatibility; this kit does not certify safety-equipment compatibility. Do not cover mandatory labels, reflective areas, visibility features, or vents.

All supplied colors are exact **sRGB**. Use `../colors/Cypress_Command_RGB.ase` for swatches. CMYK conversion must use the selected printer/substrate profile; no universal CMYK or unverified Pantone equivalents are asserted. Request a physical color sample where color matching matters.
